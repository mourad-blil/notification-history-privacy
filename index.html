Privacy Policy for Notification History Log

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Notification History Log ("the app") is published by Mourad Blil. This policy explains what the app does with your information. If you have a question about it, write to mourad.uxdesign@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The app saves your notifications on your phone only.
  • We never receive, see or store your notifications or any other personal data from the app.
  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking software.

What the app accesses

To do its job, the app reads the notifications your phone receives once you turn on notification access. For each notification this is the app it came from, its title, text and time, and any picture attached to it. Notifications can contain personal and sensitive information, such as private messages, the names of people who write to you, and one-time codes.

The app also reads the names and icons of the apps installed on your phone that have a launcher icon. It uses them only to label saved notifications and to open the app a notification came from.

How the app uses this information

The app uses your notifications for one purpose: to show you a history you can read, search, star and export. It does not use them for anything else.

Where your information is kept

Saved notifications are kept in an encrypted database in the app's private storage on your phone. Pictures attached to notifications and your recent search terms are kept in the same private storage. Other apps cannot read this storage.

The app is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your history is not copied to Google's servers or to a new phone.

What we share

Nothing. The app does not send your information to us, and it does not share it with any other company or person. There are no third parties involved.

Exports

If you choose to export your history, the app writes a file to the location you pick. That file is not encrypted, and what happens to it afterwards is up to you.

Feedback emails

If you use "Request a feature" or "Report a bug", your own email app opens with a draft addressed to us. The draft includes the app version, your Android version and your phone model, which you can remove before sending. It never includes notification content. If you send the email, we receive your email address and whatever you wrote, use them only to reply and to improve the app, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

How long information is kept, and how to delete it

Your history stays on your phone until you remove it. You can:

  • stop the app saving notifications from any app you choose
  • delete all history in Settings
  • turn off notification access in Android settings to stop all saving
  • uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, which removes everything it saved

Because we never receive your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. As described above, we do not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, we will update this policy and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
